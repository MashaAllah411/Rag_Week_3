# Acme Corp Grade Band Entitlements Matrix (v2025, effective 2025-01-01)
[APPLIES TO GRADES 1 THROUGH 7 - RESOLVED ONLY VIA EMPLOYEE RECORD]

## Grade 1 & Grade 2 (Associate / Junior Specialist)
- Travel: Economy class domestic flight only; standard hotel cap /night.
- Notice Period: 2 weeks (or statutory minimum per entity annex).
- Overtime: Eligible for 1.5x hourly overtime compensation for approved weekend work.
- Discretionary Benefits: Standard health plan; not eligible for company car or executive wellness.

## Grade 3 (Senior Associate / Specialist)
- Travel: Economy class; hotel cap /night; meal per diem /day.
- Notice Period: 30 days written notice.
- Overtime: Exempt from overtime; eligible for compensatory off (comp-off) with manager sign-off.
- Discretionary Benefits: Standard comprehensive health insurance (covers employee + spouse + up to 2 children).

## Grade 4 & Grade 5 (Team Lead / Manager / Senior Manager)
- Travel: Premium economy for flights over 6 hours; hotel cap /night; meal per diem /day.
- Notice Period: 60 days (2 months) written notice.
- Flexible Hours: Self-managed core hours with obligation to support team core hours.
- Executive Health: Annual comprehensive executive health checkup allowance (/year).

## Grade 6 & Grade 7 (Director / Senior Director / Vice President)
- Travel: Business class for all international flights and domestic flights over 4 hours; hotel cap /night; full corporate card reimbursement.
- Notice Period: 90 days (3 months) written notice.
- Sabbatical & Extended Leave: Eligible to apply for up to 6 months executive sabbatical after 4 years tenure, subject to executive committee and HRBP approval.
- Discretionary Benefits: Executive retention bonus scheme and customized relocation package.
