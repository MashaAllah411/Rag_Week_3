# Acme Corp Leave Policy 2024 (v1.0, effective 2024-01-01, expired 2024-12-31)
[SUPERSEDED POLICY - ARCHIVED FOR HISTORICAL AUDIT & RETROACTIVE CLAIMS]

## Section 1: Annual Leave Entitlement (2024)
Clause 1.1: Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (1.25 days per month).
Clause 1.2: Maximum carryover allowed into 2025 was 3 days. Any additional unused days lapsed on December 31, 2024.

## Section 2: Parental Leave Entitlement (2024)
Clause 2.1: Primary caregivers are entitled to 10 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% base salary.
Clause 2.2: Secondary caregivers are entitled to 3 weeks of paid parental leave.
Clause 2.3: Parental leave in 2024 had to be taken in a single unbroken block within 9 months of birth or adoption.

## Section 3: Sick and Medical Leave (2024)
Clause 3.1: Full-time employees receive 8 days of paid medical leave per calendar year. Medical certificate mandatory after 2 consecutive days.
