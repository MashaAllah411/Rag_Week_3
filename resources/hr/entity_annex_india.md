# Acme Corp Entity Specific Annex: India (v2025, effective 2025-01-01)
[OVERRIDES ACME GLOBAL HANDBOOK WHEREVER CONFLICT OCCURS FOR INDIA ENTITY EMPLOYEES]

## Section 1: Statutory Maternity Benefit Override (India)
Clause IN-1.1: Under the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, female employees who have worked for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding expected delivery are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for up to the first two surviving children (12 weeks for third child onwards).
Clause IN-1.2: This 26-week statutory entitlement SILENTLY WINS AND SUPERSEDES the Acme Global Handbook and Leave Policy (which states 12 or 16 weeks).
Clause IN-1.3: Medical bonus of INR 3,500 payable if pre-natal confinement care is not provided by the employer.

## Section 2: Paternity Leave (India)
Clause IN-2.1: Male employees are entitled to 15 calendar days of paid paternity leave, available up to 6 months post childbirth.

## Section 3: Statutory Notice Period & Gratuity (India)
Clause IN-3.1: Minimum notice period for resignation or termination in India is 30 calendar days for confirmed staff, unless Grade 4+ contract specifically specifies 60 or 90 days.
Clause IN-3.2: Payment of Gratuity is applicable to employees completing 5 or more continuous years of service under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, calculated at 15 days basic salary per year of service.

## Section 4: Public Holidays & Working Hours (India)
Clause IN-4.1: India entity employees receive 10 mandatory national/festival holidays plus 2 optional floating holidays per calendar year.
Clause IN-4.2: Standard working hours are 40 hours per week (Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST).
