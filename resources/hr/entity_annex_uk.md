# Acme Corp Entity Specific Annex: United Kingdom (v2025, effective 2025-01-01)
[OVERRIDES ACME GLOBAL HANDBOOK WHEREVER CONFLICT OCCURS FOR UK ENTITY EMPLOYEES]

## Section 1: Statutory Maternity Leave & Shared Parental Leave (UK)
Clause UK-1.1: Eligible employees in the UK are entitled to up to 52 weeks of Statutory Maternity Leave (26 weeks Ordinary Maternity Leave + 26 weeks Additional Maternity Leave).
Clause UK-1.2: Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP) is paid for up to 39 weeks (90% of average weekly earnings for first 6 weeks, followed by statutory rate or 90% whichever is lower for remaining 33 weeks). Acme Corp provides enhanced pay: 100% salary for first 18 weeks, followed by statutory SMP.
Clause UK-1.3: This UK statutory framework SILENTLY WINS AND OVERRIDES the Acme Global Handbook parental leave clauses.

## Section 2: Notice Periods (UK)
Clause UK-2.1: Statutory notice periods apply: 1 week notice for tenure between 1 month and 2 years; 1 additional week per completed year of service between 2 and 12 years (capped at 12 weeks).
Clause UK-2.2: Contractual notice: Grades 1-3 require 1 month notice; Grades 4-5 require 2 months notice; Grades 6-7 require 3 months notice.

## Section 3: Workplace Pension Scheme (UK)
Clause UK-3.1: All eligible jobholders aged 22 to state pension age earning above £10,000/year are automatically enrolled in the Acme UK Pension Plan.
Clause UK-3.2: Minimum contributions: Employee contributes 5% of qualifying earnings; Acme Corp matches with 4% (exceeding statutory 3% minimum).

## Section 4: Statutory Working Time Regulations (UK)
Clause UK-4.1: Maximum working week of 48 hours applies under the UK Working Time Regulations 1998, unless the employee has signed an individual voluntary opt-out agreement.
