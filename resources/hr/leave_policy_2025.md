# Acme Corp Leave Policy 2025 (v2.0, effective 2025-01-01)
[CURRENT GLOBAL POLICY - SUBJECT TO ENTITY ANNEX OVERRIDES & ADDENDUM A]

## Section 1: Annual Leave Entitlement (2025)
Clause 1.1: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (1.5 days per completed month).
Clause 1.2: Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.

## Section 2: Parental Leave Entitlement (2025)
Clause 2.1: Primary caregivers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% base salary (increased to 16 weeks effective 2025-07-01 under Addendum A).
Clause 2.2: Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave (increased to 6 weeks effective 2025-07-01 under Addendum A).
Clause 2.3: Leave may be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption (18 months under Addendum A). 30 days prior written notice required.

## Section 3: Special Discretionary Leave
Clause 3.1: Sabbatical Leave: Unpaid sabbatical leave of up to 3 months may be requested only by employees with >3 years of continuous tenure. Requires approval from Department Head and HR Business Partner (HRBP). Sabbatical approval is discretionary and cannot be guaranteed by standard policy.
Clause 3.2: Compassionate Leave Extension: Requests for compassionate leave exceeding 5 days are strictly discretionary and must be escalated to an HRBP for case-by-case assessment.
