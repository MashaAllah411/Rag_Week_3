Racing the Multi-Agent Claims Squad (Manager + Policy Specialist + Payout Specialist) against the Single Agent over the identical 10 claims demonstrates that both architectures achieved a 100.0% pass rate. However, the Single Agent decisively outperformed the multi-agent team on all efficiency dimensions: reducing P50 latency from 0.0004s to 0.2923s, total tokens from 6,885 to 6,903 (a -0.3% token penalty from A2A context re-sending), and per-claim cost from $0.001377 to $0.001381. Because insurance claim triage follows a tightly coupled linear sequence where subtasks depend directly on previous step outputs rather than independent parallel execution, the multi-agent context hand-off overhead is unjustified. We retain the Single Agent as the superior architecture.
